FAQ

Good to know.

What files does Glide open?

PDF and plain-text .txt files. PDFs get the full reader — tabs, layouts, search, bookmarks, night mode — and text files open in a dedicated reading view with adjustable size.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones, tablets, and foldable / dual-screen devices. The layout adapts to whatever screen is in your hands.

Is it a subscription?

No. Glide is a single one-time purchase. No accounts, no ads, no recurring charges.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device. Glide reads your local files and remembers your reading position — nothing is uploaded.
